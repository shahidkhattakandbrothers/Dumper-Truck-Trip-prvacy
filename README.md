Privacy Policy for Dumper Truck Trip
Last updated: October 28, 2023
This privacy policy governs your use of the software application Dumper Truck Trip for mobile devices.
What information does the Application obtain and how is it used?
•
User Provided Information: The Application obtains the information you provide when you register and use the Application. This includes financial records, contact names, and phone numbers related to your business transactions.
•
Local Data Storage: All data entered (trips, diesel, expenses, etc.) is stored locally on your device in a secure SQLite database.
•
Permissions: The Application requires access to your device’s storage to perform database backups and restores. It also utilizes "Call" and "SMS" intents to help you contact your business partners, but it does not initiate these actions without your explicit trigger.
Data Sharing and Disclosure We do not sell, trade, or otherwise transfer your business or personal data to outside parties. Your data remains on your device unless you manually perform a backup to your own storage or share reports via external apps.
Security We are concerned about safeguarding the confidentiality of your information. Since all data is stored locally, the security of your information depends on the security of your mobile device.
Changes This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time for any reason. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy here.
